## Failure injection and observation

Drop the publication response after database commit; retry the identical command. Restart between acceptance and dispatch, after dispatch before acknowledgment, and after publication before worker settlement. Replay inbound events, race duplicate submits, send a second-room activation while the first is running, reject an unsupported steer and cancel one of two concurrent agents. Inspect durable state and public messages after each case. A browser reconnect must recover the same stream without duplicate activation.

Capture actual provider public streaming separately: prewritten chunks sent by a script prove gateway updates, not incremental model output. Record at least three content revisions produced by the selected publication path before commit and verify raw provider events are absent from the public log. If adapter support is missing, document the concrete gap and implement it before claiming streaming complete.

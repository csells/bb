# Hosted fixture cleanup

The exact hosted acceptance fixture was removed on October 10 after its dialogue evidence and screenshots were exported. The [executed result](evidence/hosted-cleanup.json) records the IDs and counts; the [script](cleanup-hosted-fixture.mjs) is retained for audit, not reuse against a different fixture.

The script first compared Workshop against immutable pre-cutover backups, checked both fixture threads were idle, rejected pending or uncertain deliveries, and matched fixture messages and activations to the exported dialogue evidence. It paused only the two fixture rooms, archived their two native threads through BB's SDK, and removed the exact room rows, 20 receipt records, and the fixture guest's account and two sessions in one transaction. SDK deletion then removed the isolated native project and its threads. Only the exact fixture workspace, private credential JSON, and corresponding participation directories were removed.

Workshop's seven original messages, two agent bindings, and membership matched the backup. Its revision had advanced from 1243 at backup to 1255 before cleanup; cleanup left it at 1255. Atlas's read timestamp and thread update timestamp had also advanced before cleanup. The full Workshop rows captured immediately before cleanup remained unchanged afterward. Chris's account, password, and five existing sessions were preserved. The owner's separate credential file remained available for the root agent's final browser check.

The first dry run correctly stopped on the room revision difference. Review allowed revision and thread read metadata to advance while retaining strict history/binding checks and exact before/after preservation. A subsequent guard exposed an evidence-shape assumption: the report identifies some messages through their cause or activation IDs rather than repeating their complete message records. Cleanup now verifies those exact references. The reviewed plan also corrected an initial assumption about gateway operator configuration: the fixture guest was never an operator, so no configuration change or restart was needed.

The [older thread inventory](evidence/older-preview-thread-inventory.json) found 12 unarchived threads from the original preview, distinct from the later V2 fixtures whose documented cleanup did archive their threads. They include the original Builder/Reviewer Workshop and earlier Agent acceptance/Open discussion fixtures. Their old database bindings remain available, and two original evidence files identify two of those rooms. All 12 threads were preserved; no archival or deletion was inferred from a title or date.
